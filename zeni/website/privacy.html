Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Zeni (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, stores and protects personal information when you use our website and mobile application.

Information we collect

We collect information you provide (such as name, phone number, identification details) and technical data necessary to operate the service and assess credit applications in line with applicable Kenyan law.

How we use information

We use your information to provide the lending service, verify identity, assess creditworthiness, disburse and collect loans, prevent fraud, meet regulatory obligations, and improve the product.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information with service providers who help us operate (under strict contracts), with credit reference systems where required, and with authorities when legally obliged.

Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss or alteration.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data subject to legal and contractual requirements. Contact us at support@getzeni.ke.

Contact

For privacy questions: privacy@getzeni.ke
