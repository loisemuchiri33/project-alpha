Security & trust

Your data and your financial wellbeing come first. Here’s how we protect both.

Encryption & infrastructure

All data in transit and at rest is protected with modern encryption standards. Our systems are monitored continuously for unusual activity.

Responsible credit decisions

We use careful assessment so that offers remain affordable. We do not encourage over-borrowing.

Direct M-Pesa only

Disbursements go only to the M-Pesa number linked to your verified profile. No cash handovers, no third-party agents.

Privacy by design

We collect only what is needed to provide the service and meet regulatory requirements. We do not sell personal data.
