Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about Zeni, how loans work, and how to get started.

What is Zeni?

Zeni is a digital lending app for Kenya. We offer fair, transparent loans that are disbursed directly to your M-Pesa wallet.

How do I get the app?

Download Zeni free from Google Play or the App Store. Search “Zeni” or use the buttons on this website.

How fast do I receive the money?

After you accept an offer, funds are typically sent to your M-Pesa number within minutes.

Are there hidden fees?

No. The full cost is shown clearly in the app before you accept any loan.

Who can use Zeni?

Adults in Kenya with a valid mobile number registered to M-Pesa and who meet our credit and eligibility checks.

How do I repay?

Repayment details (including Paybill or till information) appear in the app for each loan. Always use the details shown for your specific loan.

Does timely repayment help me?

Yes. Paying on time helps build your history and can unlock higher limits over time.

Is my data safe?

We use encryption and strict controls. We do not sell your personal data. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
