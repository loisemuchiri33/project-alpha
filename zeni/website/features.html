How it works

Everything you need in one app

Zeni is designed around how Kenyans actually borrow, repay, and get paid — with M-Pesa at the centre.

Instant M-Pesa disbursement

Once you accept an offer, funds are sent directly to your registered M-Pesa number — usually within minutes.

Transparent pricing

See the full cost of the loan before you accept. No hidden fees, no last-minute surprises.

Bank-grade security

Encryption, careful access controls, and responsible data practices protect your information.

Limit that grows with you

Responsible repayment is recognised. Over time you can unlock higher amounts as your history builds.

Flexible repayment windows

Choose terms that fit how money actually moves in your week or month — not rigid bank-style schedules.

Local support

In-app help and a support team that understands Kenyan customers and M-Pesa workflows.

Made for Kenya

From the first screen to repayment, Zeni is built around M-Pesa, local phone numbers, and the realities of everyday cash flow.
