Support

We’re here to help. Most answers are in the app — and our team is ready when you need more.

Frequently asked

How do I download Zeni?

Search “Zeni” on Google Play or the App Store, or use the download buttons on this website. The app is free.

How fast do I get the money?

Once you accept an offer, funds are sent to your registered M-Pesa number, usually within minutes.

Are there hidden fees?

No. The full cost is shown clearly in the app before you accept any loan.

How do I repay?

Repayment instructions (including Paybill or till details) appear in the app. Always use the details shown for your specific loan.
