About Zeni

We exist to make fair, transparent credit available to everyday Kenyans — delivered the way they already move money: M-Pesa.

Too many people in Kenya face opaque terms, hidden costs, or simply cannot access formal credit when life demands it. Zeni was created to change that.

We combine responsible credit assessment with a simple mobile experience so that school fees, business stock, medical bills, or everyday emergencies do not become crises.

Our promise is straightforward: clear pricing, fast M-Pesa disbursement, and products designed around how Kenyans actually earn and repay.

Our focus

Everyday borrowers who need reliable, short-to-medium term credit without complexity.

Our standard

Transparency first. You always see the full cost before you accept any offer.
